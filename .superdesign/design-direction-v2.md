# 星芽 v2 design direction — Path & Pulse（路径与一次一题）

This direction SUPERSEDES the previous 糖果乐园 / candy-card look. Keep product rules. Do not copy competitor brands, characters, logos, or exclusive illustrations (no Duo owl, no Brilliant Koji, no Khan Kodi/friends, no Todo monsters, no Synthesis wordmarks).

## Product constraints (unchanged)
Chinese-first native iPhone app for ages 9–11 and parents. Oral math, practice, and local analysis stay free offline. Monthly membership is only cloud sync + AI parent summaries. Price is StoreKit displayPrice. Existing IA pieces remain: 年级, 上/下册, knowledge-map topics that route to practice / fluency / race, tabs 首页 / 报告 / 我的, must-tap 继续, timeout, correction/read-aloud. Do not invent new product features (no new AI tutor chat, no live tutor, no energy currency, no classroom roster).

## Concept
A confident learning path, not a candy stall and not a settings list.
- Home is a PATH: one recommended "下一步" as the hero with a single primary Start action; units/topics flow as a vertical journey with a clear current node, completed behind, later ones quieter. 上/下册 remains, but is secondary. Stop making every topic an equally loud candy gradient card.
- Practice is PULSE: one problem at a time, oversized expression typography, generous whitespace, a quiet keypad, one solid primary action. After submit, a decisive feedback band (correct / incorrect) and the child must tap 继续. Timeout remains visible but not decorative clutter.
- Report is ACTIONABLE: lead with 1–2 insights a parent can act on (what is slow or weak, what to practice next), then supporting charts. Not a decorative dashboard and not a manual.

## Visual language (advanced ≠ glass or gradient stacks)
- Light, calm, precise. Warm off-white / paper background, deep ink text, one confident accent for the single primary CTA and the current path node. Subject color only as a thin signal on path nodes, never full-card candy fills.
- Typography is the craft: a clear display style for titles and math numbers; readable regular/medium body; reserved use of weight. Do not set every label in heavy rounded black. Math expressions should feel typographic, not sticker-like.
- Plenty of whitespace. Large corner radii only where they help, not cute-for-cute. Soft, low shadows. No glassmorphism, no neon, no rainbow gradients, no floating candy capsules, no mascot takeover.
- Native-feeling iOS tab bar. Chinese only.

## What was learned from official App Store UI (see uploaded references)
- Duolingo Math lesson (official Duolingo iOS listing, shot with "Create this number" / 36): one short instruction, huge target, tile interaction, thin top progress, a single obvious next step. Borrow the focus and feedback tempo, not the owl or energy gem, and not language-course chrome as branding.
- Brilliant course path + feedback (official Brilliant iOS listing): a vertical topic path with quiet cards and a clear "Correct!" confirmation band; problems sit in generous white space with one visual idea. Borrow the path IA and typographic calm; do not copy isometric 3D course art or the tutor mascot.
- Synthesis skill map + practice (official Synthesis iPad listing): skills as a typographic grid of named lessons with done/locked states; practice is a large manipulative canvas with a small help affordance and a spoken prompt, not a crowded form. Borrow the "map of skills" clarity and the uncluttered problem stage; do not copy the dark starfield aesthetic, iPad-only layout, or brand.
- Todo Math home / level picker (official Todo Math listing): a few large mode tiles and a curriculum level chooser with a clear Confirm. Borrow the idea that home is a short set of clear entries, not a wall of equal cards; do not copy monsters, treasure maps, or game-currency chrome.
- Khan Academy Kids official listing was reviewed; most frames are marketing collages for ages 2–8 with unique characters. Do not use that visual world. Only the general idea that adult tools can list named tasks is noted; Xingya's report must stay a parent/child progress view, not a classroom assignment roster.

## Screens in this pass
High-quality 首页, 答题, 报告 only. Same system so they feel like one product.
