Xingya 1.0.0 (9) — mathematics-only iOS release

Historical draft, superseded before submission by Build 10. Build 9 archive, upload and processing succeeded; the user confirmed installation. Full device acceptance was not completed; these notes were not submitted.

Tapping an option now checks the answer and shows feedback and explanation immediately. A wrong choice stays visible and choices lock until Try again; repeated taps do not consume extra attempts. Correct/revealed answers retain Continue. Fill-in and multi-step answers still use Check. Scoring and mastery thresholds are unchanged.

Grades 3–9 contain 2,056 oral-math questions, preserving existing IDs and progress structure. This includes Grade 4 answer variety, signed input, and Grade 7 rational multiplication/division under More fluency practice. Mathematics supports Chinese and English. The separate Web Chinese/English subject modules are not native iOS features.

Build 6 sign-in/subscription fixes remain: fresh authorization, retained pending state, silent cancellation and verified tokens. Empty/transient StoreKit catalogs retry with backoff; reopening Account reloads, with localized Retry. A secondary refresh failure does not undo confirmed entitlement binding.

Review paths:
1. Select a grade; complete the warm-up if shown. Practice and on-device reports are free, work offline and require no account. Tap a choice to see feedback; use Try again after a first error or Continue after a correct/revealed answer.
2. Me → Account → Sign in / Create account → Continue with Apple. Alternatively use the credentials supplied in App Review Information; no password is repeated here.
3. Sign in, then Me → Account → Monthly membership. Product: com.hanwang.xingya.premium.monthly. Name and price come from StoreKit. Retry or revisit Account if unavailable.
4. After a Sandbox purchase, active membership follows server verification. Restore using the original Xingya account. Canceling purchase or restore authentication should return silently.
5. Account → Sync now and Me → Parent → remote AI summary require active membership and server access. Local analysis stays free. Membership offers Manage subscriptions, renewal terms, privacy policy and the Apple Standard EULA. Deleting the Xingya account does not cancel its Apple subscription.
6. Grade 7 → Home → More fluency practice → Mental arithmetic: multiplication and division of rational numbers → Fluency or Race. Use ± for negative answers; the sign key applies to the whole track.
