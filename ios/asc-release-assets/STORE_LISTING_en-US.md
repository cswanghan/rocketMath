## Description (4000 characters)

```
Xingya is a math practice app for students in grades 3–9. It uses timed drills to build fluency and a knowledge map for concepts and written calculation. Parents can review a mistake book and on-device analysis.

Why Xingya
• Local-first: practice is complete without an account. Progress stays on the device and works offline.
• Mastery loop: Takeoff → Orbit → Universe. After a miss, the app explains first, then asks again. You tap Continue before the next item.
• Knowledge map: organized by grade and Term 1 / Term 2, covering mental math, written calculation, formulas, and concepts.
• Parent area (on-device PIN): free access to missed items, slow items, and local analysis. With a valid monthly membership and a signed-in account, a parent can request a remote AI summary. If the remote service is unavailable, the app clearly labels the result as free local analysis and never presents it as a cloud AI result.
• Healthy use: a rest reminder appears after about 15 minutes of continuous practice.
• Optional account: sign up, sign in, and delete the account in the app. Deleting a Xingya account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Who it is for
Students in elementary grades 3–6 and middle-school grades 7–9, and parents who want to review practice together.

Language
The interface and question bank are available in Simplified Chinese and English. Switch languages in settings.

Privacy
Learning data stays on the device by default. Agreed progress is uploaded only when a signed-in member starts a sync. A remote AI parent-summary request sends sample missed items. We do not use advertising tracking. See the privacy policy for details.

Subscription
Core fluency practice, exercises, and on-device analysis stay free and work offline.
An optional auto-renewable monthly subscription, Xingya Premium Monthly, unlocks manual cloud sync and remote AI parent summaries after you sign in, purchase or restore, and membership is validated.
The subscription lasts one month and renews automatically. It is charged to your Apple ID. The price is the amount shown in the app and on the Apple purchase confirmation. It renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; after you cancel, access continues until that period ends. Manage or cancel in Apple subscription settings. Deleting a Xingya account does not cancel the Apple subscription.
Standard EULA: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
Privacy policy: https://rocket-math-4kg.pages.dev/privacy.html
Support: https://rocket-math-4kg.pages.dev/app-support.html
```
