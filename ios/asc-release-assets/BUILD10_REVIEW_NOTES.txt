Xingya 1.0.0 (10) - mathematics-only iOS release

Build 10 is selected for this version and was installed through TestFlight for device checks. Builds 8 and 9 were superseded before submission.

Choices check on tap with feedback/explanation. Wrong choices lock until Try again; repeated taps add no attempts. Correct/revealed answers retain Continue. Oral math and Race check after a 1-second editing pause. Digit/delete/sign changes restart it; blank/lone minus never submits. Reaction time uses the last edit; pre-deadline input is checked before timeout. Backgrounding/leaving cancels pending input; race time stays absolute. Fill-in/steps retain Check. Calibration, core engine, scoring and mastery rules are unchanged.

Grades 3-9 contain 2,056 oral-math questions with existing IDs/progress structure preserved, Grade 4 answer variety, signed input and an added Grade 7 rational multiplication/division entry. Mathematics supports Chinese and English. Web Chinese/English subject modules are not native iOS features.

Review paths:
1. Choose a grade; complete warm-up if shown. Free practice and local reports work offline without an account/subscription. Enter the whole oral-math answer and pause 1 second. Use Try again or Continue to acknowledge feedback where shown.
2. Me > Account > Sign in / Create account > Continue with Apple. Alternative credentials are in App Review Information, not repeated here.
3. After sign-in: Me > Account > Monthly membership. Product com.hanwang.xingya.premium.monthly. StoreKit supplies name/price; empty/transient catalogs retry with backoff. Reopen Account or tap Retry if unavailable.
4. Sandbox purchase becomes active after server verification. Restore with the original Xingya account. Authorization/purchase/restore cancellation returns silently. A secondary refresh error does not undo confirmed entitlement binding.
5. Account > Sync now and Me > Parent > remote AI summary need active membership/server access. Local analysis is free. Manage subscriptions, renewal terms, privacy and Apple Standard EULA are available. Account deletion does not cancel the Apple subscription.
6. Grade 7 > Home > More fluency practice > rational multiplication/division > Fluency or Race. The sign key supports negatives across the track.
